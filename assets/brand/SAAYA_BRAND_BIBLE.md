# Saaya Brand Bible

**Version 1.6, signed off 2026-07-06.**
Produced by a 24-agent brand exercise (audit, consumer insights, strategy panel, design panel, adversarial review) and synthesized by the CMO pass. v1.1 rewrote the foundation language (mission, vision, values, narrative, boilerplate, campaign pool) out of corporate register after founder review, via a 3-writer, 3-judge Opus panel. v1.2 added the fearless-life register (the brand's payoff is her unhesitating yes, felt everywhere, preached nowhere) and the founder's personal vision. v1.3 replaced the vision line and the founder's opening after cold-read testing. v1.4 simplified both flagship lines for universal first-read clarity: same energy, same meaning, explicit subject. v1.5 made the vision the category endgame (the last safety app) and promoted "Go anywhere without thinking twice." to the campaign pool. v1.6 resolved five open production decisions: "Shakti" (no H) spelling verified against AP government usage, App Store subtitle drops its period in-field to fit 30 chars, social handles set canonical (@saaya.app / @saaya_app), and publish gates written into the X plan and launch pack. Strategy substance and the visual system are unchanged. This is the single source of truth for how Saaya looks, speaks, and behaves. The website, deck, app, App Store listing, police dashboard, and every social post answer to this document.

Writing rules baked into this document and binding on all copy: no em dashes, no "actually / essentially / ultimately / seamless / elevate / delve", no exclamation marks on consumer surfaces, no fear bait, no paternalism.

## 1. Who we are for

**Persona (fixed, everyone designs for her):**
She is 20 to 25, in Hyderabad or Bengaluru, or in Vizag, Vijayawada, Tirupati, Guntur. She earns her own money or is close to it, and she is often the first woman in her family to move through the city this freely. She knows the independence is real but provisional: it was granted and it can be withdrawn, so she optimizes to never look reckless. Her day runs a quiet background process of vigilance. She screenshots the plate before the cab moves, sends live location to her mother, walks the lit route instead of the short one. These rituals are already witness behaviors (3 in 4 women report having used a phone to get out of a situation that felt risky*). Each ritual taxes her autonomy, because the same feed that protects her also reports on her. Her phone is autonomy object and leash at once; the Apple Watch matters because it is the only safety surface nobody else can see. Her peak-need moment is solo travel, when the mother, sister, and friend who normally hold her are three hundred kilometres away. She does not want to be rescued. She wants to keep moving. Her job for us: "let me stop noticing."

*\*Research phrasing uses "unsafe situation"; that word stays in the citation and never in copy.*

**The core emotion Saaya owns: Accompanied calm.**
The off-guard exhale of a woman who is finally not keeping watch alone. Her real burden is low-grade vigilance, not fear. The feeling Saaya sells is the relief of setting that vigilance down while a witness she chose holds it. Every decision stays hers.

**And what the calm buys: the fearless yes.**
The end feeling of the whole brand, on every surface, is that she can live at the same size as anyone who never runs the safety math. Plans decided by want, never by risk. This is the quiet politics of the brand: named here once, and never said out loud in copy. We never write "empowerment", "fearless", or any comparison to men; we show the yes instead (the trip taken, the late shift accepted, the shortcut walked). Reassurance is delivered as license, not as protection.

**Category position.**
The market splits into pink (girly-victim aesthetics) and police-blue or khaki (state authority). Violet sits between them and reads as neither. The incumbents sell fear with the aesthetics of authority. Saaya sells competence with the aesthetics of intimacy. That gap is the brand.

**Brand style (the one-line answer).**
Editorial dark minimalism, the Umbra system. Minimalism is the spine, warmed by editorial type and light-based motion so it never reads clinical. Neo-brutalism is quarantined to the SOS screen; glassmorphism's glow is rebuilt as light on solid surfaces, never frosted panels. Not maximalism, not brutalism. The style gets more minimal as the stakes rise. Full argument in section 4.

## 2. Foundation

> ### Mission
> **Let your phone do the noticing.**
> It runs the safety checks you already run, holds your live trail for the people you chose, and answers only to you.

> ### Vision
> **Safety apps shouldn't have to exist. We're building the last one.**
> Until that day, Saaya checks the streets for you, so your plans never start with "is it safe?"

> ### The founder's vision (personal, signed)
> **"I want to watch my friend go wherever she wants, no second-guessing. The trip, the late shift, the city she hasn't seen. To live her life to the fullest, and never let the doubt of safety talk her out of it."**
> Abhishai, founder. Used on the About page, in decks, and in press; the brand vision stays canonical for everything else.

### Core values (4)

| Value | The line |
|---|---|
| **It walks beside you** | If a feature decides for you, it does not ship. Nothing overrides you, including us. |
| **Calm is the feature** | We never scare you into installing, never dress a calm moment as a crisis. |
| **On device, then gone** | Recordings stay on your phone and delete themselves the moment you're safe. |
| **Plainer as stakes rise** | The higher the danger, the fewer the words and the bigger the button. |

**In practice (the non-negotiables these values force):**
- SOS sits off the center of the app and the story; every surface leads with the passive layers; success is measured by ambient daily use, not SOS triggers.
- No family dashboard, ever. Witnesses are named by her and reach the trail through a no-install link she opened, never a console handed over her.
- Copy says "higher-risk area", never "unsafe". No caption or screen opens with a crime statistic, and crime counts stay off the ember palette. No streaks, badges, or gamified safety.
- On-device first. Police see risk density, never a person, until SOS. Precise location leaves the phone at exactly one moment (SOS). The Watch is her private channel that no stranger and no family member can see. DPDP compliance is designed in.
- Every line of copy is tagged with its stakes-ladder rung. Human words, never product words: home, mom, walk, buzz, deleted.
- Every asset passes the un-mockable test: if a 22-year-old could screenshot it to mock it, it dies.

### Brand narrative (canonical, in voice)

Saaya means shadow. Not the kind you fear. The kind that walks beside you.

You already do the work. You screenshot the plate. You share your location with your mother. You take the lit road over the short one. You watch. You never stop watching. That noticing is a full-time job nobody hired you for.

Saaya takes the shift. It reads the risk in the streets around you. It holds your live trail for the people you chose. If something goes wrong, it acts.

Everything stays on your phone. Nothing leaves without you. The recording deletes itself the second you get home safe.

You were never asking to be rescued. You wanted someone to notice with you. Now something does.

Go say yes.

### Personality

**The steady friend who notices everything, says little, and moves the moment it matters.**

Wry in daylight, silent at SOS. Too calm, too competent, too adult to parody.

### Tone of voice: The Steady Friend (the one who stays on the line)

Principles:
1. **Presence over alarm.** Calm is the safety feature, not a mood. State what is true, plainly. Never manufacture fear.
2. **Beside her, never above her.** "For your protection" is a banned construction.
3. **Calm scales with stakes.** Wit lives at the caption, none survives at SOS. Tag every line with its rung.
4. **Recognition before instruction.** Mirror the habit she already runs, then name Saaya as the automation. Never open with a feature list or a crime stat.
5. **Human words, not product words.** Home, mom, walk, buzz, deleted. Never "geofence", "guardians notified", "utilize".
6. **Friendship shown by restraint.** The product voice never says "I". Privacy is delivered as care, not a compliance clause. (Carve-out: the social/creator voice may use a peer "I", as in "every girl I know". Saaya-as-speaker never does.)
7. **The payoff is her yes.** Reassurance shows up as the life she says yes to (the trip, the late shift, the shortcut), never as protection language and never as the words "empower" or "fearless". If a line makes her feel guarded, it failed; if it makes her feel free to plan, it worked.

- Pronouns: "you" on consumer surfaces; "she/her" only in B2G and investor material.
- "Shakti" (no H) is the one spelling in all police material, verified 2026-07-06 against AP government usage (CM Naidu's March 2025 launch, Shakti Suraksha War Rooms, Shakti Teams). The app's own Play Store title is styled "SHAKTHI"; we match the government program spelling for a government audience and do not mirror the store transliteration.
- Numerals are the evidence device (19,952 / 0 of 12 / 3,401): keep them big, keep them off the alarm palette.
- Social handles (canonical): Instagram **@saaya.app**, X **@saaya_app** (X forbids dots, so the underscore form is the match). Founder build-in-public runs on **@abhishaii**. Register/confirm @saaya.app and @saaya_app before the launch pack ships; if either is taken, pick one consistent alternate and update all bios and the launch pack together.

### Boilerplates (locked)

**Consumer:** Saaya is a safety app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It reads the risk around you, holds your live trail for the people you chose, and steps in during an emergency. Everything stays on your phone and deletes itself when you're home safe. The quiet friend that walks beside you.

**Investor / B2G:** Saaya is a prevention-first women's safety system for iPhone and Apple Watch. Where every prior app waits for her to press a button, Saaya triggers itself: background risk detection built on real crime data, silent watch check-ins, no-install witness trails, and automatic SOS with evidence, on-device first and DPDP-compliant by design. Witnessed, not rescued.

## 4. Visual identity: the Umbra system

**Direction: Umbra, editorial dark minimalism.** Umbra is the fully shadowed core of a shadow; Penumbra is its soft violet edge. The design operationalizes the name.

### Philosophy verdict (minimalism vs glassmorphism vs neo-brutalism)

- **Minimalism is the spine (about 80% of the system).** Premium restraint is what this audience reads as "this respects my intelligence and my data", and it holds the category gap between safety-pink and police-navy. Its risk is drifting clinical, so personality lives in type and motion, never ornament: a literary serif italic on emotional words, halos and trails that breathe, and a warm aubergine dark instead of a cool near-black.
- **Neo-brutalism is wrong as a brand, right for one screen.** Raw borders and shouting type are manufactured anxiety. Its one virtue, ruthless legibility, is quarantined to the SOS rung: plain, still, literal type and nothing else.
- **Glassmorphism is seductive over a dark map and mostly a trap.** Frosted panels put a must-read-in-one-glance banner at the mercy of whatever map luminance sits behind it. We keep the feeling and kill the liability: **the aura renders as light, not glass.** Emissive glow on solid opaque surfaces (risk halos, breadcrumb trail, watch pulse ring), never a translucent panel with text on it. The entire glass budget is one high-opacity smoked scrim over the live map, used for contrast.

The stakes ladder governs pixels exactly as it governs copy: full editorial character in marketing, trimmed at the banner, zero at SOS.

### Surface modes (explicit, no ambiguity)

| Surface | Base mode |
|---|---|
| iOS app, watchOS | **Umbra dark-first** (the map is the product) |
| Landing page, App Store, decks | **Paper light-first**, with dark map screenshots and dark chapter sections embedded |
| Police dashboard (B2G) | **Paper light-first**, data-dense register (section 5) |

**Map risk ramp (single-hue violet, salience corrected):**
Higher risk must read brighter and denser, never darker, on a dark map.

| Level | Color | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Ambient / low | Deep Violet `#5B21B6` | Low-opacity veil, no bloom |
| Elevated | Risk Veil `#8B5CF6` | Medium opacity, soft breathing halo |
| High | Risk Bright `#C4B5FD` | Highest opacity, widest bloom (10.8:1 on Umbra Void) |

Rules: driven by opacity and luminance buildup, never a red-amber-green traffic light, never amber. Risk level always carries a **non-color channel**: labeled bands on tap cards (Low / Elevated / High) and halo density, so the map survives grayscale, deuteranopia, and sunlight.

### Motion

- Core easing `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`, 200 to 280ms for sheets and nav; map camera 400 to 600ms. Nothing bounces or overshoots.
- Signature ambient motion: the living shadow as light. Halos breathe on a 4 to 6s loop (opacity 0.4 to 0.7, scale 1.0 to 1.04), a calm heartbeat, never a strobe. The breadcrumb trail marches its dashes over about 2s.
- Haptics carry the message when the screen must stay invisible: one soft tap for a watch check-in, one soft warning tap with the risk banner, a firm sustained triple pattern for SOS engaged, a quiet settle-tick for home-safe. No alarm audio by default (sound could endanger her).
- **The SOS screen never animates decoratively.** Ember field and text appear in an 80ms hard cut. The only motion allowed: the functional hold-to-stop progress and one steady 1s recording heartbeat.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and Low Power: halos become static glows, the trail simply appears, no meaning is carried by motion alone.
- The risk banner triggers max screen brightness on display, and an Ink-on-Paper high-luminance variant exists for direct-sunlight reading.

### Iconography, icon, imagery

- **App icon (evolve, don't bulldoze):** keep the aubergine radial ground, violet gradient, and pin silhouette. **Drop the checkmark** (reads verified/done/banking, off-thesis). Replace with the name made visible: a soft violet form casting a Penumbra aura with a short dashed breadcrumb trail beneath it. Flatten the candy-gradient and heavy glow into refined single-source depth, enlarge the glyph to about 72% of canvas, thicken details to survive 60px and favicon sizes.
- **Wordmark:** custom drawn lowercase "saaya" on Fraunces bones, the two a's sharing a stroke as an owned ligature (two forms walking together). One lockup, one color per context: Ink on light, Moonlight or Saaya Violet on dark. Always lowercase.
- **UI icons:** humanist line icons, 1.75 to 2px stroke, rounded caps, single weight; active state is a Violet Lift fill, not a color change.
- **Map language (the connective tissue):** custom dark aubergine map style, streets as dim hairlines, labels in Ash Violet, risk as breathing violet halos (emission, never translucency), her position a Saaya Violet dot with soft aura, the witness trail a dashed glowing Violet Lift breadcrumb, destination pins with no checkmark. Text over the map always sits on an opaque scrim.
- **Illustration ("cartography as poetry"):** contour lines, route paths, breadcrumb constellations, abstract city grids, calm violet line art. Needs no photoshoot; built for the 30-day X campaign.
- **Photography:** real women 20 to 25 moving through the city at the violet hour, self-possessed, documentary, long cast shadows as the recurring motif. Never staged fear, never a woman clutching her phone, never damsel or queen imagery.
- **Banned visual vocabulary:** shields-with-checkmarks, alarm bells, sirens, red outside SOS, pink, locks and chains.

## 5. B2G register (police dashboard and government material)

The consumer brand is fenced off from government tone, and the government surface gets its own served register (not improvisation):

- **Inherits:** the violet anchor (Deep Violet on Paper), Hanken Grotesk (or system stack), evidence numerals with tabular figures, the us-vs-them card grammar, Ink/Paper/Paper Shade surfaces.
- **Drops:** Fraunces italic and all emotional-emphasis styling, all wit and campaign lines, breathing halos (dashboard risk maps are static density, no animation), the intimate second person.
- **Adds:** data-first density (tables, filters, timestamps), operator ergonomics (persistent filter state, high-contrast light UI for control-room lighting, keyboard-first flows), authority through precision: data-provenance footers (NCRB and source citations), DPDP compliance line on every export.
- **Language:** she/her in aggregate, never an identified individual until an SOS handoff. "Risk density", "zone", "response" are correct here; they remain banned on consumer surfaces.
- **Never:** khaki or police-navy theming, consumer taglines on operator screens, Saaya Violet as small text on white (use Deep Violet).
