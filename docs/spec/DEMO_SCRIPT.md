# Saaya Lite - Demo Video Script
Three minutes maximum, per the brief. Shot list, timings and exact narration.
Recorded on E9. Do not improvise on the day.

## Setup before recording

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Device | mid-range Android, not a flagship. It should look like her phone. |
| Demo speed | **ON**, divisor 6, ladder runs 15 / 10 / 10 s |
| Banner | the demo banner **stays visible**. Never hide it. |
| Zone | `dwaraka_police_station`, HIGH tier |
| Simulated hour | 04:05, `NIGHT_DEEP` |
| Favourite | one synthetic contact, "Amma", synthetic number |
| Console | open on a second screen, live, filter Last 24 hours |
| Capture | screen recording at device resolution, plus a browser capture of the console |

## Rules for the recording

- **Show the demo banner.** Never crop or hide it. A judge who spots a concealed demo mode
  discounts everything else.
- **Never speed up the video to fake responsiveness.** Demo speed is a labelled product
  setting, and that is honest. Editing the footage to look faster is not.
- Say the government numbers **as the governments published them**, and cite on screen.
- No music over the SOS section. Silence is stronger and it is respectful.
- Show the real device, real fingers, one hand. This is a one-handed product.
- End on the disclaimer, not on the logo. Honesty is a scored criterion.

## Fallbacks

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| Live console update fails on camera | pre-record the console segment separately and cut to it. Never fake it with a mockup. |
| Screen recorder drops frames on the map | record the map segment at 30 fps and the rest at 60 |
| Telugu unverified by the founder | record in English only and say the app ships English and Telugu, rather than showing unverified strings |
